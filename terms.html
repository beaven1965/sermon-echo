1. About Sermon Recorder

Sermon Recorder (also called Sermon Echo) is a web app that records sermons, lectures and meetings, turns them into text, and gives you highlights, a takeaway, a quiz and Bible study tools. It is run by Beaven Bermudez in the Philippines. By using Sermon Recorder, you agree to these Terms.

2. Free and Premium

  • Free: recording, the full transcript, highlights, the takeaway, your Library (with titles, speakers, events and topics), printing, saving, and Listen with your phone's own voice.
  • Premium (₱250 for 30 days): Quiz for Students (multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, matching, short answer and essay), Bible Concordance, Bible Commentary with Voices across the ages, Bible Authors, Bible Genealogy, Bible & Science, Quotable Quotes, Illustrated Bible Stories, and AI translation and AI narration of the highlights in 37 languages.
  • AI uses: each Premium payment includes 5 AI uses per calendar month. One use is translating one recording's highlights into one language, including listening to it with the AI voice (up to 2 listens per use). Opening a translation you already made is free. The monthly uses reset on the 1st of each month (Philippine time) and do not carry over. The app shows how many uses you have left.
  • Refills: when your uses run low, you can buy 5 more AI uses for ₱50. Bought uses are used after your monthly 5, and they stay until your Premium code expires. They do not carry over to a new code.
  • Features and limits may be improved or adjusted over time. We will not reduce what you already paid for during your current 30 days.

5. Recording responsibly

Please only record talks you are allowed to record, such as with the permission of the speaker, church or school. Respect the privacy of the people you record, and do not use recordings or transcripts to harm, mislead or embarrass anyone.

6. AI results and accuracy

  • Transcripts, highlights, takeaways, translations, quizzes and Bible study results are made with artificial intelligence (OpenAI for transcription and the AI voice, and Claude by Anthropic for writing and translation). They are usually accurate but can make mistakes. Please check important details, names and Bible references before you share or teach them.
  • In Voices across the ages, each person's view is a short summary in our own words, not a direct quote.
  • Sermon Recorder openly presents a biblical, creationist worldview. Other views are described fairly and respectfully.
  • Links to maps, pictures, charts and YouTube open other websites. We do not control their content.

7. Your privacy

  • Your recordings and your Library are saved on your own phone or computer, not on our servers. If you clear your browser, they may be lost, so download any recording you want to keep.
  • To make a transcript, your recording is sent securely to OpenAI's transcription service. To make highlights, translations and quizzes, the text is sent securely to Anthropic's Claude service. We do not keep a copy of your recording or its text.
  • We keep only what is needed to run Premium: your access codes, the names you give family codes, and how many AI uses and refills were used.
  • We handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).
